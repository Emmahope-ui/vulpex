Cleaner campuses, stronger communities

Vulpex is an emerging waste management and environmental sustainability company. We provide efficient, reliable and sustainable solutions in Ghana, starting with universities and tertiary institutions.

We promote responsible disposal, recycling and waste valorization, which means turning waste into valuable resources. The result is better sanitation, a protected environment and new economic opportunities across Ghana and Africa.

    Environment

    Less litter, less landfill, healthier surroundings.

  • 🧼

    Sanitation

    Consistent collection that keeps spaces safe to live and study in.

  • 💼

    Opportunity

    Jobs and markets built on what others throw away.

What we do

Select a service to see how it works.

🚛

Waste collection and management

Scheduled and on-demand pickups with clearly labelled bins for general, recyclable and organic waste. We serve halls of residence, lecture blocks, canteens and offices.

  • Flexible schedules, including 24/7 emergency pickups
  • Bin placement and sorting guidance on site
  • Regular reports on volumes collected
🌱

Organic waste valorization

Food scraps and plant waste are too useful to bury. We convert them into compost and soil enrichers that farms and gardens can use.

  • Canteen and market food waste diverted from landfill
  • Compost for campus gardens and local farmers
  • Less odour and fewer pests where waste is handled
⚡

Waste to energy partnerships

We work with energy partners who turn non-recyclable waste into power and heat, so what cannot be reused still serves a purpose.

  • Partnerships with energy and biogas providers
  • Reliable supply of sorted feedstock
  • Open to institutions that want to host projects
🏪

Local market opportunities

Recovered plastics, paper, metals and compost find buyers close to home, creating income for collectors, artisans and small businesses.

  • Buy-back links for recyclable materials
  • Work for local collectors and sorters
  • Products and materials that stay in the local economy

Questions

Who do you serve?

We start with universities and tertiary institutions in Ghana, and also work with other organisations and communities.

Can I request a pickup at night?

Yes. We work 24/7, so call or message us at any hour.

What happens to the waste you collect?

Organic waste is composted, recyclables are sold on, and what is left goes to waste-to-energy partners.
