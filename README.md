# vulpex

VULPEX is an emerging waste management and environmental sustainability venture focused on providing efficient, reliable, and sustainable waste management solutions in Ghana, with an initial emphasis on universities and tertiary institutions. The company aims to promote responsible waste disposal, recycling, and waste valorization by transforming waste into valuable resources. Through innovative waste management practices, VULPEX seeks to improve sanitation, protect the environment, create economic opportunities, and contribute to sustainable development across Ghana and Africa.
